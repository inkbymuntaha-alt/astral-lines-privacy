Astral Lines – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Developer: Musha Games · Contact: inkbymuntaha@gmail.com

Astral Lines (“the app”, “the game”) is a puzzle game for adults (18 and over) published by Musha Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. We keep it as small as we can: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and we do not run a server that stores your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, collection, settings, the name you type for the leaderboard, items you have unlocked) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted. The “Copy backup code” feature creates a code that you can save yourself; we do not see or store it.

To time optional daily reminders, the app stores on your device when you last opened the game, the hours of day you usually open it (your last 7 opens), your daily streak and whether reminders are on. Reminders are off until you turn them on. They are created by your device; no information about them is sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can reopen your choices at any time in Menu → Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices (shown where required).
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or account details. Google tells the app whether a purchase succeeded so the item can be unlocked, and lets you restore one-time purchases after reinstalling. Google’s handling of your payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Fonts

The game loads its text fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your device contacts Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and device information as part of a normal web request. The game works with default fonts if this connection is not available.

5. Sharing and challenges

When you tap Share, the app opens the Android share sheet with a message about your result, and you choose who receives it. A friend-challenge link may contain the name you typed for the leaderboard (up to 14 characters), the date and your score. Only share it with people you want to see that information. Do not use your real full name or personal details as a leaderboard name.

The “Sky Rivals” on the leaderboard are simulated players created by the game; they are not real people’s data.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, fonts and in-app purchase information.
  • Notifications: only if you choose daily reminders.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is handled by Google (AdMob, Google Play, Google Fonts) as described above. We may disclose information only if required by law.

9. Retention, deletion and your rights

We keep no personal information about you on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the data stored on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. For data held by Google, use your Google account’s privacy controls. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data (such as access, correction or deletion); because we do not hold your data, these are exercised with Google. You can still contact us with any request at the address below.

10. Security

Your progress stays on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandbox. Purchases and ads run through Google’s services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app’s Google Play listing.
